# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro, deployed as a static site. A local service business site lives or dies on search ranking and mobile load speed. Astro ships zero JavaScript by default and produces plain HTML pages that are cheap to host anywhere. It can add small interactive pieces (the estimate form, a booking widget) only where needed. Hosting target is undecided.

## Users

Homeowners of all ages in Los Angeles County and Orange County, California. Many arrive on a phone, often right after noticing a leak, missing shingles, or storm damage. Others are planning a replacement and comparing contractors. Their job is to find a roofer they can trust and get someone out to look at the roof, soon.

## Product Purpose

The marketing website for a local, family-owned residential roofing company. It turns visitors into leads through three actions, all first-class:

- request a free estimate (form),
- call now (tap-to-call),
- book an appointment online.

Success means more qualified estimate requests, calls, and booked inspections from the two-county service area.

## Positioning

A family-owned roofer with a long local history, backed by manufacturer certifications and warranties. It also offers fast and emergency response and financing. Those four things together are the pitch: established and accountable like a big firm, but local and quick to show up.

## Operating Context

- Residential roofing work: repairs, replacements, storm and leak damage, emergency response.
- Service area: Los Angeles County and Orange County, CA. Local SEO for cities within both counties matters.
- Leads arrive by phone, estimate form, and online booking. The booking tool or provider is undecided.
- Financing is offered. The provider and terms are undecided.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Company name, phone number, address, license number, years in business, and hours: **not yet provided**. Use clearly marked placeholders and never invent values.
- Specific manufacturer certifications and warranty terms: confirmed to exist but **not yet provided**. Placeholders until supplied.
- Emergency service hours or 24/7 coverage: confirmed as a strength, exact terms **not yet provided**.
- Financing terms: **not yet provided**. Never state rates, APRs, or "0% down" claims without real terms.
- California contractor license number must appear once provided (CSLB requirement for advertising).

## Brand Commitments

Family-owned identity and long local history are core to the voice. A logo exists but has not been added to the repo yet.

Standing preference (chosen in the first direction round): the site follows the category standard for a residential roofing contractor (photo-led hero, estimate form, trust badges, services, reviews, financing, service area), executed at the craft level of the best national and top local roofing sites. It must still not read as a generic contractor template: quality comes from real proof, precise type, and local specificity, not a different visual concept.

## Evidence on Hand

The owner has all of the following, but none of it is in the repository yet:

- customer reviews,
- project photos (before/after and finished jobs),
- company logo, license, and proof of insurance,
- manufacturer certification badges.

Until the real files arrive, build with visible placeholders. Never fabricate reviews, star ratings, review counts, job counts, years in business, or certifications.

## Product Principles

1. **Get the call.** Every page makes calling, requesting an estimate, and booking obvious and one tap away on mobile.
2. **Prove it, don't claim it.** Trust comes from real reviews, photos, license, and badges, not adjectives.
3. **Local first.** Speak to LA and Orange County homeowners specifically; local relevance drives both trust and search ranking.
4. **Fast on a phone.** Visitors are often stressed and on mobile; pages must load instantly and read easily.
5. **Honest urgency.** Emergency and storm messaging is welcome, but never fake scarcity or pressure tactics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The audience spans all ages, including older homeowners. Target WCAG 2.2 AA: readable type sizes, strong contrast, large tap targets, and forms that are simple and clearly labeled.
