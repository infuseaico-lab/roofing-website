    Import from CSV

    First row must be headers. Recognized columns: Client, Listing, Post On, Review, Image URL, Post Date, Poster Name, Review Link, Status, Paid. Paid (whether the poster was paid) accepts Yes/No. Export from Excel or Google Sheets as CSV.
